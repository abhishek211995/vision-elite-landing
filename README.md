# P Visiion Luxury Launch

Build an ultra-luxury, conversion-optimized single-page portfolio landing page for "P Visiion Interiors" targeting high-net-worth clients in Pune and beyond.

Incorporate the brand identity, typography, color palette, and business details from the attached letterhead, brochure pages, and business card (Sucheta Alve, +91 8805 028 765, pvisiion@gmail.com, 310 Khinvasara Trade Center, Datta Mandir Road, Wakad, Pune, 411033; Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/pvisiioninteriors).

Key specifications:
- Palette & Aesthetic: Obsidian dark, warm cream (#F5F5F0), soft greige, with cognac warm accents, muted teal, and rich walnut. Elegant serif typography (Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display) paired with clean Inter sans-serif. Glassmorphism, subtle micro-interactions, razor-thin borders, and high-fashion editorial spacing.
- Sticky Header: Logo text "P VISIION INTERIORS", smooth-scroll anchors (The Gallery, Design Philosophy, The Blueprint, Client Reviews), and "Reserve a Consultation" CTA button.
- Cinematic Hero: Headline "Engineering the Future of Ultra-Luxury Living", subhead, dual action CTA ("Unlock Private Portfolio" opening lead drawer & "Explore Aesthetics" scrolling down), and framed glassmorphic showcase card.
- Interactive Lead Engine: "Bespoke Space Cost & Vision Architect" 4-step wizard (Typology, Design Language, Scale/Footprint, Reveal Form with Pune/PCMC/Global location selector) delivering an instant Lookbook download state and direct WhatsApp consultation launcher.
- Asymmetrical Bento Grid: Finished concepts with hover interactions, category pills, and an interactive Before/After image comparison slider.
- 3-Step Turnkey Process: Timeline highlighting The Vision & Digital Twin, Bespoke Material Curation, and Rigorous Turnkey Execution (with warranty & Vastu notes).
- Proof & Reputation: 4.8/5 rating, Pune & PCMC active projects stats, structural transparency, and curated client testimonials.
- Luxury Footer: Location map/address card in Wakad Pune, contact links, and official Instagram link. Responsive mobile drawer navigation included.
